Combat weapon SFX (shoot*.wav)
================================

Source pack: Kenney "Digital Audio"
  https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio
  License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Processing (revival build):
  - Converted OGG → mono 44.1 kHz WAV
  - High/low-pass filter, light compression
  - Reduced gain, short fade-in/out, tempo variants per weapon
  - Output: public/sfx/combat/shoot*.wav

Original 1999 client WAVs remain under public/archive/extracted/sound/
(for archive fidelity; not used for in-game fire SFX anymore).
