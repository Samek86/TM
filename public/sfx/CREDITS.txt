Combat weapon SFX (shoot*.wav)
================================

Procedural aircraft-style missile / cannon one-shots (revival build).
  - Layered ignition thump + swept pink-noise whoosh + engine hiss
  - Variants per weapon class (cannon / dart / cruise / nuke / cloud)
  - ffmpeg loudnorm → mono 44.1 kHz WAV
  - Generator: scripts/render-missile-sfx.mjs
